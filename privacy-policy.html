LingoMix—Video Subtitle Player Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 August 2026

LingoMix lets users open compatible video links, select available video, audio and subtitle languages, play the result, and optionally send it to a Google Cast device.

Data handled

No LingoMix account is required. The app does not include advertising or developer-operated analytics. The app processes URLs supplied by the user and media metadata returned by the relevant content host. Language, theme, quality and subtitle-style preferences are stored locally on the device.

Network and third parties

To resolve and stream a link, the app sends requests to the content host identified by that link. When casting, selected media URLs and playback metadata are sent through Google Cast to the Cast device selected by the user. Those third parties process data under their own privacy terms. The developer does not operate a server that receives the user's video links or viewing history.

Temporary data

Android, the media player, the content host or the Cast device may temporarily cache media for playback. LingoMix does not create a permanent personal viewing profile. Users can remove temporary media through the app and can clear all app data through Android settings.

Permissions

Internet and network-state access are used to resolve links, stream media and discover Cast devices. Notification permission, when requested on supported Android versions, is used only for Cast playback controls. Denying notification permission does not grant the app any other access.

Children

LingoMix is a general-purpose media utility and is not directed specifically to children. The app does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

User choices and deletion

Preferences remain on the user's device. They can be reset in the app or deleted by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Content responsibility

Users must have permission to access and play the media they open. LingoMix is independent from and is not endorsed by third-party content providers.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the app's data handling changes. Questions can be submitted through the project's public support page: GitHub support.
